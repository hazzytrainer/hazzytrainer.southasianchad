Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you fill out our application form, we collect the information you provide: your name, contact details (email and/or phone), and your answers to the application questions (age range, location, goals, and related fitness background).

How we use it

  • To review your application and determine whether the program is a fit.
  • To contact you about your application and next steps.
  • To improve our programs and messaging.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used only for the purposes above.

Data retention

Application data is kept for as long as needed to evaluate applications and operate the business, then deleted on request.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us and we will respond promptly.
